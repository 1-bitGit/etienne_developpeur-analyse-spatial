My Skills

GIS Programming

I started learning to code because I grew frustrated by the rigid environment of traditional Geographic Information Systems. Since then, my focus has shifted from simple online cartography and visualization to what is going on behind the scenes.

Database Management

Data management is one of those aspects of the backend that felt dry and totally unattractive to me at first. Over time, it became one of my favorite things because of how powerful the right tools and systems can be.

Process Automation

I took an interest in automation out of curiosity for its potential in processing and analyzing bigger data volumes. Learning how things work under the hood helps me increase efficiency, which opens the door to more possibilities and new insights.
